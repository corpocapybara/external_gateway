  - "After a refresh whose retried call is still rejected (email ErrAuthRejected or http-rest 401), or a refresh that itself fails (non-200 or transport error), a further rejected call for the same workspace/env within the cooldown makes no token-endpoint call and returns the rejection error, saying the refresh was skipped (cooldown).
