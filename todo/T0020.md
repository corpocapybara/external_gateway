Found in the T0014 design review (2026-11-11). executeHttpConnector (internal/server/server.go ~788-809) turns every param that is not in the path or body template into a query-string argument; ValidateParams checks declared params only. So an agent can add e.g. notifyUsers=false or overrideScreenSecurity=true to jira.edit's PUT. Decide: reject undeclared params (403 validation) for every op, or per op. Owner question.
