Found in EG0019 review (correctness note, 2026-11-11). CheckConstraints (internal/policy/registry.go, jql_scope case) leaves projects nil when constraint["projects"] is e.g. a number or an unknown $workspace.* name, so JQLScopeConstraint.Check falls back to workspace.Policy.JiraProjects and, if that is empty, scopes nothing. Pre-existing, not caused by EG0019.

Refinement notes (2026-11-12): out of scope, by design: a known $workspace.* name whose policy list is empty stays unscoped (the existing "no configured scope" behaviour). A list of non-strings (e.g. [1,2]) loads as "a list" but CheckConstraints already fails closed on it (registry.go:630). checkJQLScope can follow the validator in jira_key_scope.go:140. Only jql_scope users today: $workspace.jira_projects (example and live ops) and the literal [SRTHP] (thp), so failing closed breaks none.
