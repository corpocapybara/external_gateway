**An expired GitLab PAT fails silently until a call returns `invalid_token`** (2026-11-04). There were 7
`invalid_token` answers on 10-15/16 (0ec2bbfd, cf2c8d4f), and sessions switched to the browser. Report the
token's `expires_at` (`GET /personal_access_tokens/self`) in `/capabilities` or `gitlab.whoami`, and warn when it
is less than 7 days away.

**Refined 2026-11-12.** The owner already gets this warning: the tray app (`cmd/egw-desktop/checker.go` ~307)
checks every 30 min and notifies 14 days before expiry, on expiry and on rejection (since 2026-10-20, after the
10-15/16 incidents). Owner decision 2026-11-12: add the agent-side warning anyway. `tokencheck.checkGitLab`
already reads `expires_at`; `/capabilities` reports nothing about credentials.
